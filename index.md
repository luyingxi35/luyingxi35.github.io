## Research Interests
My research focuses on LLM-based embodied agents for Physical AI. I am interested in how agents can act in the physical world, construct environments and tasks for learning, and continually improve through interaction.

- <span style="color:darkred"><strong>LLMs as Embodied Policies:</strong></span> Enabling large language models to make grounded decisions and generate executable actions and policies through closed-loop interaction with their environments.
- <span style="color:darkred"><strong>Agentic Environment and Task Generation:</strong></span> Building agents that autonomously construct, validate, and refine interactive simulation environments and tasks for embodied learning and evaluation.
- <span style="color:darkred"><strong>Self-Improving Embodied Agents:</strong></span> Developing agents that learn from interaction and execution feedback to improve their policies, accumulate reusable skills, and generalize to new tasks.
